# 💳 PayStream Payment Failure Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses payment transaction data for PayStream to identify patterns behind payment failures, data quality issues and changes in business performance.

The analysis focused on understanding where payment failures were occurring, identifying trends across different merchant and payment categories, and translating the findings into practical business recommendations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Investigate payment failure patterns within the dataset
- Identify data quality issues before analysis
- Analyse payment performance across different merchant and payment categories
- Examine weekly trends and changes in payment performance
- Identify areas contributing to increased payment failures
- Provide clear findings and recommendations to support business decision-making

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualisation

## 🔍 Analysis Approach

### 1. Data Preparation
The dataset was reviewed and cleaned before analysis to identify missing, inconsistent or incorrect values that could affect the reliability of the results.

### 2. Payment Failure Analysis
Payment performance was analysed to understand where failures were occurring and which areas of the business were most affected.

### 3. Trend Analysis
Weekly payment trends were investigated to identify changes in performance over time and periods where payment failures increased.

### 4. Business Analysis
The results were interpreted from a business perspective to understand the potential impact of payment failures and identify areas requiring further investigation.

## 📈 Key Findings

- Payment performance showed noticeable changes across the period analysed.
- A significant deterioration was identified around Weeks 9–12.
- Certain merchant and payment categories showed higher levels of payment failure than others.
- The analysis highlighted areas where payment performance required further investigation.
- Data quality checks were important for ensuring that conclusions were based on reliable information.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, PayStream should continue monitoring payment failure rates and investigate the areas showing the greatest deterioration.

Particular attention should be given to changes in performance over time and differences between merchant and payment categories. Improving data quality and regularly monitoring payment KPIs would also help the business identify emerging problems earlier.
